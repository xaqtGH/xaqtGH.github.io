---CREDITS ROOM---

Button 1: Get into the idiot room, or have someone else go there. Once in there, hit the Harry Enfield poster (acting as button 1) to unlock the second button.
Button 2: Get into the church room, and damage the cross (acting as a button). Once this is done, immediately head to the water pool in the North Korean room and get to the bottom of the center. A part of the floor will then dissapear after a short delay, taking you to the credits.
