---CHARA ROOM---

To unlock this room, you will need to hit all the cheeses in the map with bullet weapons, as well as hit 2 invisible buttons. These can be done in any order.

Cheese 1: In the room where Heavy is dead.
Cheese 2: Behind Cheese 1, in the trade_coni room, behind the tanks.
Cheese 3: Above the rafters in the central room of the map.
Cheese 4: In the room with the Muppet TV, next to the bookcases up top.
Cheese 5: In the room in front of RED spawn, on a corner.
Button 1: Burgerpants' nose on the painting in the central room.
Button 2: Simply damage Undyne in front of the BLU spawn.

If all of these cheeses and buttons are hit, a distinctive sound effect will play, and the wall behind the Asriel poster in the multi-coloured room will deactivate. Hit Ainsley and wait on the metal grate.


---CREDITS ROOM---

THIS CAN ONLY BE UNLOCKED AFTER THE CHARA ROOM CHEESES AND BUTTONS ARE HIT.

Invisible buttons must be hit (does a sound effect once a button is successfully hit), and order matters:

Button 1: Shoot the mosaic ceiling in the middle of the Snoop Dogg room.
Button 2: On an area in the Temmie idiot room button activation area, where the wall and ceiling meet.
Button 3: Above the galactic corridor entrance, in the judgment hall room.
Button 4: In the Ruins room, hit the upper corner of the ceiling next to the vine, in the corridor, next to Toriel.
Button 5: Behind the "IDIOTS ONLY" sign in the Merasmus room, NOT the idiot room tp located on the treadmill.
Button 6: On a corner next to the yellow-haired Superjail twins.
Button 7: On the circle of the window to the left of the hallway entrance of Button 3. Once this button is hit, crouch under the ramp.
